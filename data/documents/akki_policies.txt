# AKKI LATEST COLLECTIONS - STORE POLICIES & CUSTOMER ASSISTANCE

## 1. Shipping & Delivery Policy
- **Free Delivery**: All orders placed on AKKI LATEST COLLECTIONS qualify for **100% Free Shipping** with zero delivery charges across all pin codes in India!
- **Dispatch Time**: Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.
- **Delivery Timeline**: Metros and tier-1 cities receive deliveries within 2 to 4 business days. Regional and rural pin codes take 4 to 6 business days.
- **Courier Partners**: We partner with BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and India Post Speed Post for express and secure handling.
- **Order Tracking**: Once dispatched, an SMS and email notification with an AWB tracking number is shared with the customer.

---

## 2. Payment Methods & Cash on Delivery (COD)
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available nationwide on orders up to ₹15,000. Customers can pay in cash or via UPI QR code directly to the courier agent upon arrival.
- **UPI**: Instant 0-fee payments supported via Google Pay (GPay), PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM UPI.
- **Cards**: All major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Diners Club, and Amex).
- **Net Banking**: Supported across 50+ public and private Indian banks.
- **Security**: 256-bit bank-grade SSL encrypted checkout.

---

## 3. 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement & Return Policy
- **7-Day Window**: We offer a generous 7-day replacement guarantee starting from the date of package delivery.
- **Eligible Scenarios**:
  1. Size mismatch in ready-to-wear dresses.
  2. Received damaged, defective, or incorrect product.
  3. Noticeable color deviation from catalog display.
- **Condition for Return/Replacement**: The product must be unwashed, unused, and in its original packaging with all tags and invoice intact.
- **Pickup**: Free doorstep reverse pickup is arranged by our courier partners.

---

## 4. How to Shop & Use Akki Collections Website
1. **Browse Catalog**: Use the top Category bar (`All`, `Saree`, `Dress Material`, `Dress`, `Cotton Dress`) to filter the collections.
2. **Filter & Sort**: Select subcategories, sort by price (Low to High, High to Low), customer rating, or newest arrivals.
3. **View Product Details**: Click **"🔍 View Details"** on any product card to see high-resolution images, fabric specifics, customer ratings, and discounts.
4. **Add to Cart & Checkout**: Click **"🛒 Add"** or **"Buy Now"**, review your order in the Cart page, and complete checkout.
5. **Ask Akki Assistant**: Click the **"💬 Ask Akki"** floating assistant at the bottom-right corner anytime for fabric recommendations, outfit matching, price inquiries, and order help.
